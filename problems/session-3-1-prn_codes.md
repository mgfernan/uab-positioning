# Exercises: PRN codes

## Problems

### 1. Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR)

Given the following mathematical representation of a linear feedback shift register (LFSR):

$$
1+x+x^4
$$

1. Make the block diagram representation of the LFSR
2. What is the theoretical maximum length of the sequence generated by this LFSR?
3. What is the maximum length of the sequence generated by this LFSR if we initialize it with `1111`?

***

## Answers

### 1.

1. The block diagram representation of the LFSR is shown below:
```{mermaid}
flowchart LR
    R1 --> R2["R₂"]
    R2 --> R3["R₃"]
    R3 --> R4["R₄"]
    R4 --> OUT["Output"]
    XOR["XOR ⊕"] --"feedback" --> R1["R₁"]

    R1 -. "tap" .-> XOR
    R4 -. "tap" .-> XOR

    style R1 fill:#e8f4ff,stroke:#0066cc,stroke-width:2px
    style R2 fill:#e8f4ff,stroke:#0066cc,stroke-width:2px
    style R3 fill:#e8f4ff,stroke:#0066cc,stroke-width:2px
    style R4 fill:#e8f4ff,stroke:#0066cc,stroke-width:2px
    style XOR fill:#fff,stroke:#000,stroke-width:2px
```

2. The theoretical maximum length of the sequence generated by this LFSR is $2^4-1 = 15$ bits, since the degree of the polynomial is 4.
3. The actual length can be computed by simulating the LFSR with the initial state `1111`. The sequence generated will be:

| Step | R1  |  R2  |  R3  |  R4  | Feedback |
|:----:|:---:|:----:|:----:|:----:|:--------:|
| 1    |  1  |  1   |   1  |   1  |     0    |
| 2    |  0  |  1   |   1  |   1  |     1    |
| 3    |  1  |  0   |   1  |   1  |     0    |
| 4    |  0  |  1   |   0  |   1  |     1    |
| 5    |  1  |  0   |   1  |   0  |     1    |
| 6    |  1  |  1   |   0  |   1  |     0    |
| 7    |  0  |  1   |   1  |   0  |     0    |
| 8    |  0  |  0   |   1  |   1  |     1    |
| 9    |  1  |  0   |   0  |   1  |     0    |
| 10   |  0  |  1   |   0  |   0  |     0    |
| 11   |  0  |  0   |   1  |   0  |     0    |
| 12   |  0  |  0   |   0  |   1  |     1    |
| 13   |  1  |  0   |   0  |   0  |     1    |
| 14   |  1  |  1   |   0  |   0  |     1    |
| 15   |  1  |  1   |   1  |   0  |     1    |
| 16   |  1  |  1   |   1  |   1  |     0    |

`010110010001111` is the sequence generated, which has a length of 15 bits. Therefore, the maximum length of the sequence generated by this LFSR when initialized with `1111` is 15 bits.

Note that step 16 returns to the initial state, confirming that the sequence has cycled through all possible states except the all-zero state.
